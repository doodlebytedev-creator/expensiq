Privacy by design

Your finances stay personal.

Expensiq works without an account. Your entries are stored on your device, and we don't run servers that collect your financial records.

On your phone

Your entries and settings live in the app's private storage. No account, ads or tracking.

Your own Google Drive

If you enable backup, Expensiq uses a private app folder in your Drive. We can't see your backup.
